# Assumption Ledger

## ESSENTIAL

1. Costly downstream information production.
2. Incomplete appropriability / noncontractibility of the information-production effort.
3. The information producer's appropriable downstream footprint shrinks with `η` (`ω_S' < 0`).
4. The information is reusable through upstream product improvement outside the originating route (`D_R' > 0`).
5. The expanding route's weighted marginal system return is sufficiently high for the coordinated response to be positive.

## REGULARITY

- `ω_R' > 0` for the interpretation of downstream reallocation toward `R`.
- `D_S',D_R',g' > 0`.
- Sufficient concavity / cost convexity for unique interior optima.

## WELFARE-ONLY / BENCHMARK DISCIPLINE

- quasilinear utility;
- consumer masses `1-h(η)` and `h(η)` with `h' > 0`;
- real distribution costs `c_S > c_R ≥ 0`;
- non-strategic common price.

## TRACTABILITY

- benchmark linear product improvement `x=βe`;
- benchmark quadratic effort cost `ke²/2`;
- benchmark quadratic utility / linear demand.

## SUPPORTING ORGANIZATIONAL

- the information-producing route has higher real distribution cost in the retention benchmark;
- a private retention threshold exists when the local under-retention result is invoked.

## MOTIVATIONAL

- specialist/dealer terminology;
- appliances and named institutional examples.

## KILLED / PROHIBITED AS DRIVERS

- Stage-2 raising-rivals'-costs / foreclosure mechanism;
- strategic wholesale-price manipulation;
- direct retail-service utility as the central mechanism;
- bargaining or hold-up as an added mechanism;
- P4 hump-shaped retention as a general contribution.
