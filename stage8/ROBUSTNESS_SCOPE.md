# Robustness Scope

Verified before Stage 8:

- nonlinear route shares/activity weights;
- nonlinear learning functions;
- nonquadratic effort costs;
- general IFT comparative-static formulation;
- a separate quasilinear utility/welfare microfoundation;
- numerical counterexample searches in alternative parametric families.

Canonical description of the main theorem:

**ROBUST UNDER CONDITIONS.**

Do not describe the result as unconditional or fully general. The coordinated sign requires the weighted marginal-return condition (G), and the private sign requires a shrinking appropriable footprint plus the relevant concavity conditions.
