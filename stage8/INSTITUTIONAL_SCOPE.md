# Institutional Scope

## Supported primitive

Primary-source work completed at Stage 7 supports the chain:

**field/service information → upstream product/quality/design improvement.**

Motivating and cross-industry examples include:

- appliances;
- automobiles;
- agricultural machinery;
- heavy equipment;
- medical devices.

## Boundary

Institutional evidence illustrates and defends the economic primitive. It does **not** establish the full causal model or prove the exact elasticity from downstream rents/sales to information-production effort.

The canonical theory therefore uses **information-producing downstream node** rather than treating any named dealer network as the theory itself.
