# Prediction Scope

Frozen Stage-7 predictions only:

1. Private information effort at the information-producing node may decline as activity reallocates away from it.
2. Manufacturer/system value or support for reusable information may rise at the same time.
3. The divergence should be larger where field information is more reusable across routes or products.
4. Upstream investment in diagnostic, reporting, or engineering-feedback infrastructure may rise even while the originating node's sales decline.
5. The sign reversal should weaken or disappear when information has no upstream cross-route use.

These are interpretation/falsifiability predictions, not an empirical design. No additional predictions are authorized at Stage 8.
