## 3. Canonical Research Question

**MINOR SEMANTIC CLARIFICATION — FROZEN.**

How does reallocating downstream activity away from an information-producing node affect private information production when that information improves an upstream product used across routes, and when can private and system-optimal information effort move in opposite directions?

## 4. Canonical Economic Problem

A downstream node can lose the return it privately appropriates from information production as activity reallocates away from it, even while the same reusable information becomes more valuable elsewhere in the vertical system.

## 5. Canonical Mechanism

**Information Appropriability under Downstream Reallocation**: shrinking appropriable own-route return versus expanding cross-route system return to reusable information.

## 6. Players

`M` upstream producer; `S` information-producing downstream node; `R` alternative downstream route; consumers explicit only in the welfare benchmark.

## 7. Timing

`η` is exogenous/common knowledge → `S` chooses noncontractible `e` → `x=g(e)` → the improved product is used across routes and payoffs realize. Retention is a separate supporting benchmark only.

## 8. Information

Information is a productive input generated by effort, not revelation of an existing state. No bargaining, signal game, or effort-contingent transfer.

## 9. Core Friction

Incomplete appropriability of noncontractibly produced downstream information.

## 10. Canonical General Model

`q_S=ω_S(η)D_S(x)`, `q_R=ω_R(η)D_R(x)`, `x=g(e)` with `ω_S'<0`, `ω_R'>0`, `D_i'>0`, `g'>0`.

Private: `π_S=m_Sω_SD_S(g(e))-C(e)`.

Coordinated: `J=A_Sω_SD_S(g(e))+A_Rω_RD_R(g(e))-C(e)`.

## 13. Equilibrium Concept

Core theorem: **conditional optimal-effort comparison** — unique private optimum of `S` versus unique coordinated optimum, conditional on exogenous `η`. It is not a Bertrand/Nash pricing game.

Supporting retention benchmark: two-stage sequential organization choice followed by `S`'s effort; SPNE terminology is appropriate only there.

## 14. Private Effort Characterization

IFT gives `de^P/dη<0` under `ω_S'<0` and the private SOC.

## 15. Coordinated Effort Characterization

IFT gives `de^C/dη>0` iff condition (G) holds.

## 16. Main Theorem

**Theorem 1 — Information-Incentive Divergence under Downstream Reallocation:** under the frozen primitive and regularity conditions, condition (G) implies `de^P/dη<0<de^C/dη`.

## 17. Main-Theorem Proof Strategy

Implicit Function Theorem on the private and coordinated FOCs; private sign comes from the shrinking appropriable footprint, coordinated sign from the weighted marginal-return condition.

## 18. Supporting Proposition / Corollary

Without cross-route product-improvement value (`D_R'=0`), the positive coordinated response disappears. Under one-for-one reallocation with equal product response, condition (G) reduces to `A_R>A_S`.

## 19. Microfounded Benchmark

Quasilinear utility, exogenous route incidence, real distribution costs `c_S>c_R`, `x=βe`, and `C=ke²/2`. It disciplines welfare; it is not the source of the main theorem.

## 20. Consumer Surplus / Welfare

## 21. Effort Ordering

## 22. Widening-Wedge Results
