# Timing and Information

## Core comparative-static problem

1. The downstream-reallocation index `η` is realized and is common knowledge. It is exogenous to the core theorem and is **not** a strategic choice variable.
2. The information-producing downstream node `S` observes `η` and chooses noncontractible information-production effort `e ≥ 0`.
3. Effort generates upstream product improvement `x = g(e)`.
4. The improved product is used through both downstream routes and payoffs are realized.

## Information structure

- `η` is common knowledge.
- `e` is chosen by `S` and cannot be directly contracted upon.
- Information is **produced by effort as a productive input**; it is not merely revelation of a pre-existing demand state.
- The resulting upstream product improvement can create value through both `S` and the alternative route `R`.
- No asymmetric demand-state signal, information-revelation game, bargaining over information, or effort-contingent transfer is part of the canonical theory.

## Supporting retention benchmark

Only for the supporting organizational result, an upstream retention decision may precede `S`'s effort choice. Retention is not the strategic driver of the main theorem.
