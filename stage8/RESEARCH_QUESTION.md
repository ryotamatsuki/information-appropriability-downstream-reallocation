# Canonical Research Question

## Freeze decision

**MINOR SEMANTIC CLARIFICATION — FROZEN**

Stage 7.5 used the phrase “when can private effort move opposite to system value?” Stage 8 replaces that phrase with the economically equivalent but more precise “when can private and system-optimal information effort move in opposite directions?” No model content changes.

## English

> **How does reallocating downstream activity away from an information-producing node affect private information production when that information improves an upstream product used across routes, and when can private and system-optimal information effort move in opposite directions?**

## Japanese

> **複数の販売経路で利用される上流製品を改善する情報を川下主体が生産しているとき、取引がその主体から別経路へ移ることは私的な情報生産をどう変え、どのような条件で私的な情報生産量とシステム全体で望ましい情報生産量が逆方向に動くのか。**

The question is application-independent and does not rely on Panasonic, appliances, dealers, or strategic retail pricing.
