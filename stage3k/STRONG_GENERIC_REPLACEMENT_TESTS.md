The Stage 6-J failure showed that additive benchmarks are too weak. Each Stage 3-K candidate was therefore attacked with the strongest natural non-information analogue.

## K1 — contest replacement

Candidate loop:

`information effort -> signal -> posterior -> business share -> effort`.

Replacement:

`productive effort -> noisy score -> prize/business share -> effort`.

The posterior can be replaced by a score without changing the equilibrium contest logic. Any theorem about stronger future allocation incentives increasing/decreasing effort is therefore not information-specific unless it exploits a Bayesian property absent from score contests. No such property was identified.

## K3 — controlled-state replacement

Information version:

`mu_t -> a_t -> e_{t+1} -> signal law -> mu_{t+1}`.

Generic version:

`x_t -> a_t -> e_{t+1} -> x_{t+1}=G(x_t,a_t,e_{t+1})`.

Let `a` increase/decrease the marginal productivity of `e`. Any candidate strategic-complement/substitute switch, investment trap, or threshold can be recreated by a deterministic transition `G`. The Bayesian posterior is not structurally necessary to the proposed theorem class.

## K4 — data/productivity feedback replacement

Information version:

`belief -> product/price action -> participation -> data -> future belief/quality`.

Generic version:

`quality -> price/action -> participation -> capability stock -> future quality`.

This is the established data-feedback / learning-by-doing topology. Replacing statistical data by a productive stock preserves the feedback and potential tipping/path-dependence.

## K6 — organizational investment replacement

Information version:

`mu_t -> d_t -> e_t -> y_t -> mu_{t+1} -> d_{t+1}`.

Generic replacement:

`x_t -> d_t -> e_t -> x_{t+1} -> d_{t+1}`,

where `x_t` is relationship-specific capability or accumulated organizational knowledge. Organization `d_t` changes the downstream party's investment return; effort changes next-period capability; capability changes optimal integration/outsourcing. Outsourcing traps and dynamic boundary reversals can arise without inference. Anderson–Parker-type learning effects make this analogue economically natural rather than artificial.

## K7 — R&D contest replacement

Competitive experiments can be replaced by competing R&D projects whose effort shifts success/product-quality distributions and future rents. Strategic substitution/complementarity, redundancy, and diversity incentives survive.

## Binding lesson

A future candidate cannot pass by showing that information affects a state. It must exploit a mathematical feature of posterior inference that cannot be recreated by an endogenous deterministic state with a sufficiently flexible but economically standard transition function.
