## B — review selection and vertical adaptation

### Acemoglu et al. (2022)

The key prior result is the endogenous review-selection effect: who purchases/reviews depends on current information, so review distributions are selected and learning speed is endogenous. This is the binding predecessor for the statistical likelihood object.

Citation-neighborhood searches returned substantial review-selection and online-learning work, but the inspected models generally place the selection decision with consumers or the same firm that later adapts, rather than a distinct downstream intermediary choosing the screening rule in anticipation of a separate upstream adaptation action.
