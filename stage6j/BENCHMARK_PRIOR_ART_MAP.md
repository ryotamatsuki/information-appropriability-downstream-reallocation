## Prior-art implications

1. Acemoglu et al. establishes the review-selection / latent-composition learning component.
2. Chen–Du–Lei establishes that price can adjust review selection bias and informativeness.
3. Yan et al. establishes dynamic price/quality adaptation around review volume and learning precision.
4. Generic economic quantity-quality models establish that an optimizing selector can trade fewer units for greater average quality.

The Stage 4-J benchmark suite proves its exact Bayesian identities but does not establish that the B-P1 comparative static is unique to information.
