# Citation-Chain Audit — Stage 6-J

Search cutoff: 2026-09-03.

This audit is complete for the top threat neighborhoods used in the Stage 6-J decision, but is not a claim of globally exhaustive literature coverage.

## 1. Endogenous review-selection lineage

### Acemoglu, Makhdoumi, Malekian & Ozdaglar (2022)

*Learning From Reviews: The Selection Effect and the Speed of Learning*, Econometrica 90(6), 2857–2899.

Model-level relevance:

- Bayesian learning from online reviews;
- heterogeneous users decide whether to purchase/review;
- the set of purchasing users is endogenous to available information;
- this endogenous population creates the paper's selection effect;
- the paper compares learning dynamics and speed under rating systems.

Forward neighborhood reviewed for work combining reviews with dynamic firm decisions, including Yan et al. (2026) and recent review-system work.

## 2. Price × review-informativeness lineage

### Chen, Du & Lei

*The Interactions of Customer Reviews and Price and Their Dual Roles in Conveying Quality Information*, Marketing Science 44(1), 155–175.

Priority record from accessible sources:

- received 2022-10-20;
- working-paper/SSRN circulation by 2024;
- accepted 2024-06-20;
- published online 2024-08-07;
- issue 2025.

Model/result relevance:

- customer reviews suffer selection bias;
- price and reviews jointly convey quality;
- price may be adjusted to mitigate selection bias and make reviews more informative;
- high-quality seller can use price strategically in the presence of review-selection bias.

This is the strongest literature threat to the Stage 4-J arrow `commercial screening/price -> selected review informativeness`.

## 3. Review-driven adaptation lineage

### Yan, Bian, Perera & Guan

*Dynamic Pricing and Product Quality with Review-Driven Learning*, Production and Operations Management 35(7), 2659–2681 (2026).

Priority record:

- author accepted / first published online 2025-12-09;
- online-first revision 2026-01-19;
- issue published July 2026.

Model/result relevance:

- two-period pricing and product-quality decisions;
- early review volume and valence determine learning precision;
- the firm uses reviews to refine product quality and later pricing;
- without quality refinement, a lower introductory price boosts review volume/learning;
- with refinement, optimal initial price can be higher or lower depending on conditions.

This is the strongest recent threat to a proposed link from early commercial policy through reviews to later adaptation.

## 4. Vertical information lineage

### Hu, Sun, Zheng, Chen & Huang (2021)

*Committed or contingent? The retailer's information acquisition and sharing strategies when confronting manufacturer encroachment*, International Journal of Production Economics 240, 108294.

Model/result relevance:

- retailer strategically acquires consumer quality-preference information;
- retailer may share information;
- manufacturer quality/sales decisions respond to the information structure;
- acquisition timing changes incentives and induced quality.

Related foundation includes Guo (2009), Guo & Iyer (2010), and Guan–Mantrala–Bian (2019).

## 5. Self-selection / review-distribution neighborhood

Hu, Pavlou & Zhang (2017) and adjacent review-selection work document acquisition and reporting self-selection as drivers of observed review distributions and firm responses. Recent Bondi–Rossi work provides a broader review-data-generating-process map including tastes, price, purchase, and reporting selection.

## 6. Endogenous data / sampling neighborhood

Liu & Li (2024) explicitly study first-party data acquisition methods with response-rate, selection-bias, and data-quality tradeoffs. Athey–Levin provide a general value-of-information benchmark. These sources reinforce that endogenous information demand and data quality are established objects, though no exact B-P1 prior was verified.
