## Decisive result-level comparison outside prior literature

A deterministic analogue satisfies all equilibrium-sign objects needed for B-P1 without satisfying the information objects R3–R5:

\[
n(c)=1-c,\quad q(c)=\kappa t^2c^2,\quad Q(c)=n(c)q(c).
\]

Then `Q(c)=I(c)` and the selector's FOC is identical to Stage 4-J. The fixed-quality benchmark `q(c)=\bar q` is identical to the fixed-likelihood volume benchmark. Thus the B-P1 sign reversal itself is not diagnostic of Bayesian learning.
