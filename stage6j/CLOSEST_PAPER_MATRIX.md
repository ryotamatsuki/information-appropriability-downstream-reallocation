## Strongest overall literature threat

**Chen–Du–Lei** is the closest paper on the exact commercial arrow `price/screening -> selected reviews -> informativeness`. Its published abstract explicitly states that price may be adjusted to mitigate selection bias and make reviews more informative.

## Strongest endogenous-selection threat

**Acemoglu et al. (2022)** is the canonical Bayesian selection-effect threat: the types who purchase and review depend on available information, changing the review-generating population and speed of learning.

## Strongest review-driven-adaptation threat

**Yan et al. (2026)** jointly analyze review volume/valence, learning precision, dynamic pricing, and post-launch quality refinement; the introductory price may move either up or down when refinement is available.

## Strongest vertical-IO threat

**Hu et al. (2021)** and the earlier Guo / Guo-Iyer channel information family establish strategic links between downstream information and manufacturer quality/pricing decisions, but they do not use an ordinary commercial cutoff to create a latent selected-sample likelihood.

## Literal prior-art conclusion

No inspected paper qualifies as EXACT PRIOR ART for B-P1.

That is not the canonical Stage 6-J disposition because the theorem fails the stronger generic-replacement and one-agent tests independently of literal prior art.
