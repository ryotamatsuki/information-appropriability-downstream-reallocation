## Exact-prior conclusion

`EXACT PRIOR ART`: none verified.

## Strongest threats

1. **Habermacher (2025)** — strongest economics-level structural threat: organizational authority changes private returns/influence to information, produces specialization/concentration, and can cause decision makers to lose perspective.
2. **Moresi (2000)** — strongest old information-acquisition analogue: two-dimensional quality, endogenous specialization/research effort, and higher research effort can coexist with worse allocation efficiency.
3. **Multitask incentive literature** — strongest immediate-corollary threat once q=1 is label-stripped.
4. **A-optimal design** — establishes that `tr(P^{-1})` and the value of directional balance are standard information-geometry objects.

The project is therefore not killed by literal single-paper identity; it is killed by a stronger Stage-6 finding that the only proved headline theorem collapses at q=1 to a generic separable concave effort-allocation comparative static whose economics are already standard.
