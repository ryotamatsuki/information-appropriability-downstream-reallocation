## Exact claim searched

An exogenous shift in private returns across two costly information producers can raise equilibrium aggregate signal precision while increasing Bayesian posterior quadratic loss when producers supply non-collinear information about a multidimensional state; the divergence is impossible in the collinear benchmark.

## Search outcome

No exact predecessor was located that reproduces the full Stage 4-H game and H-P1 literally. However, the decisive kill does not depend on a missing-paper inference. Algebraic label stripping of the frozen q=1 theorem shows that it is a generic separable concave multitask allocation result, while multiple established literatures already contain the two substantive pieces: incentive-driven effort misallocation and information specialization/concentration. The exact-condition algebra is new to this notation but does not create an independently new economic interaction.
