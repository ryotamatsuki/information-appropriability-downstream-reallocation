# Theory Change Policy

After the Stage 8 PR is merged, the merged specification is the canonical theory baseline.

## Substantive theory changes

The following require a `THEORY_CHANGE_RECORD`, identification of the earliest affected research stage, rollback to that stage, and re-running all downstream gates that depend on the change:

- research question;
- players or active decision makers;
- timing;
- information structure;
- core friction / appropriability structure;
- utility or demand primitives that affect results;
- real-cost interpretation;
- main theorem or its conditions;
- welfare proposition;
- organizational implication;
- contribution claim;
- closest-literature distinction.

## Editorial changes

The following are normally editorial if economic content is unchanged:

- prose tightening;
- theorem numbering;
- exposition ordering;
- typographical corrections;
- variable renaming accompanied by an explicit notation map.

## Rollback principle

Never repair a substantive inconsistency silently in a later stage. Roll back to the earliest stage whose conclusion would have been affected by the proposed change.
