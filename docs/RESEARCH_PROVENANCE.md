# Research Provenance

## Research seed

The project originated from the paper:

> 「家電量販店と系列店 ～家電の流通についての分析～」

written by Ryota Matsuki approximately fifteen years before the current project, for a university seminar competition during his third undergraduate year.

## Required provenance wording

This source is a **third-year undergraduate seminar-competition paper (大学3年生時のゼミナール大会用論文)**.

It must not be described in project records as a graduation thesis (「卒論」「卒業論文」).

## Research transformation

The current project is not a prose revision of the old paper. The original vertical-alignment / wholesale-pricing mechanism was audited and subsequently killed as a novelty claim. The surviving research branch was rebuilt through the research-paper-workflow into a different general mechanism concerning incomplete appropriability of downstream-generated information under downstream reallocation.
