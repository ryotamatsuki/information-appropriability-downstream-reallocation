## H — Diagnostic / informational diversity

### Proposed full-game distinction

- two downstream nodes incur information-producing effort;
- nodes generate signals about different directions/components of an upstream product/problem state;
- upstream manufacturer performs Bayesian updating and chooses product/design action;
- downstream reallocation changes the source composition and private returns to information production;
- welfare depends on posterior geometry, not only scalar information volume.

| Prior | Evidence maturity | Relevant overlap | Missing relative to proposed H | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Xiong, Li & Lang (2025), GEB | PUBLISHER ABSTRACT VERIFIED | Correlated consumer information; monopolist chooses whom to acquire information from; correlation changes targeting | No downstream intermediary information effort/appropriation; no vertical product-design response to heterogeneous diagnostic directions | `COMPONENT OVERLAP` / strongest result-level threat |
| Myatt & Wallace (2019), JET | PUBLISHER ABSTRACT VERIFIED | Multiple costly signals with clarity/correlation; strategic network use/acquisition | Different players/objectives; no downstream reallocation or upstream product-design game | `COMPONENT OVERLAP` |
| Migrow & Squintani (2023), JPE Micro | PUBLISHER ABSTRACT VERIFIED | Correlated acquisition and sharing across divisions | Organizational incentive-design game, not downstream reallocation/product-design feedback | `COMPONENT OVERLAP` |
| Board & Meyer-ter-Vehn (2024), AER, “Experimentation in Networks” | FULL MODEL VERIFIED | Network structure can crowd out experimentation and alter total information | Dynamic experimentation among networked agents, not vertical diagnostic source composition/product design | `COMPONENT OVERLAP` |
| Gao, Xie & Zhou (2015), Journal of Operations Management | PUBLISHER ABSTRACT VERIFIED | Supplier technological diversity -> novel information sharing -> buyer innovation | Empirical/institutional, not a solved equilibrium model | `MERELY RELATED` / institutional support |

No inspected single prior reproduces the proposed H player set, objectives, downstream effort decisions, reallocation, multidimensional diagnostic signals, manufacturer Bayesian design response, and proposed concentration/retention results by direct relabeling or parameter restriction.

Classification: **`POTENTIALLY NOVEL — STAGE 4 TEST REQUIRED`**.

This is not a novelty certification. Stage 4 must first establish a nonmechanical theorem, after which Stage 6 must re-kill the actual solved proposition against these and any newly discovered priors.

## Whole-game conclusion

Eight candidates are rejected because a close full mechanism already exists or the information object collapses to generic investment/effort. H is the only candidate with a sufficiently distinct minimal strategic-feedback graph to justify Stage 4. J is retained as a runner-up but not authorized for simultaneous development.

The Stage 3-I result is therefore a mechanism-search `GO`, not a claim that H is publication-grade novel.
