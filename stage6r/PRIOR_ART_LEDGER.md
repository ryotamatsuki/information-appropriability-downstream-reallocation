## Proposition classifications

### P3 — private effort down / coordinated effort up

Literal theorem-level classification: **`STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE`**.

Contribution disposition: **KILLED**.

Reason: Xu et al. (2022) already generate the same qualitative joint sign pattern in a richer dual-channel effort-free-riding model. The current theorem's additional generality is the generic IFT condition that the coordinated mixed partial be positive; it introduces no new player, state, strategy, timing, or feedback. A functional-form/general sign characterization of a known economic pattern is not sufficient under the workflow's novelty standard.

### P2 — cross-route use necessary; one-for-one condition `A_R>A_S`

Classification: **`ABSORBED — IMMEDIATE COROLLARY`** at the reduced-form level.

Contribution disposition: **KILLED AS CONTRIBUTION**; remains mathematically valid support.

Reason: once effort benefits are split across routes, integrated desired effort depends on the relative marginal route value. Cross-channel free-riding models already embody the requirement that the other channel benefit from the provider's effort. `A_R>A_S` is the expanding route having the larger integrated marginal return.

### P1 — tractable retention threshold

Classification: **`COMPONENT OVERLAP`**.

Disposition: remains robustness/special case only; cannot be promoted to rescue the paper.

Status: **KILLED, unchanged**.

## Maximum defensible conclusion

The empirical/institutional statement that downstream field/customer information can improve upstream innovation remains plausible and supported, but **no proposition-level theoretical contribution survives in the frozen static game**.

The current contribution branch therefore fails Stage 6-R.
