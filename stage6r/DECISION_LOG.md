### Correction to Stage 11

The Stage-11 statement that Pu's centralized objective yields the current positive coordinated sign by simply reversing the channel-margin ordering was based on a partial derivative holding `p_r` and `p_d` fixed. Pu's actual centralized model chooses both prices jointly with effort and reports that centralized effort falls with free riding in its deterministic baseline. No admissible published Pu parameter region with the opposite centralized sign was verified.

Therefore:

`Pu 2017 != verified immediate-corollary kill of P3`.

This correction does not revive the contribution.

### New decisive evidence

Xu, Tang, Lin & Lu (2022), IJPE 249:108506, use effort-created demand terms `(alpha-beta)e` in the offline channel and `beta e` in the online channel, with `0<=beta<=alpha`. Their full text reports:

- decentralized effort decreases with free riding;
- centralized effort increases with free riding when consumers prefer the online channel;
- centralized effort decreases when consumers prefer the offline channel.

Thus the qualitative P3 sign reversal already occurs in an admissible published dual-channel effort-free-riding model.

## Why the frozen theorem does not survive as a new general theorem

The frozen general condition (G) is exactly the condition that the coordinated effort objective's mixed partial with respect to `e` and reallocation be positive. Under concavity, that is equivalent to `deC/deta>0`. The one-for-one reduction `A_R>A_S` says that moving effort-created value toward the route with larger integrated marginal return raises desired integrated effort.

That is a useful abstraction, but after Xu et al. it is not a new economic interaction. The frozen model contains no strategic upstream response, no dynamic information stock, no adoption/disclosure/ownership decision, and no separate information state that affects equilibrium beyond the monotone transformation `x=g(e)`.

## Diagnosed deficiency

**The current model treats information as a monotone effort-generated common input but gives information no independent strategic state or response margin, so after label stripping the core game collapses to established cross-channel effort free riding and its private/system incentive wedge.**
