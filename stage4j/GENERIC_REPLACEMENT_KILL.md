# Generic-Replacement Hard Kill

## Adversarial deterministic analogue

The generic replacement is intentionally stronger than a constant-output benchmark.

Keep the same consumer screening rule `z>=c`. Let every selected type generate nonnegative deterministic continuation productivity `v(z)>=0`. Then total continuation output is

`H(c)=E[v(z) 1{z>=c}]=integral_c^1 v(z) dz`.

The average productivity of selected customers may rise with `c`; arbitrary type heterogeneity is allowed. Nevertheless total output satisfies

`H'(c)=-v(c)<=0`.

A retailer with continuation stake `rho` and objective

`c(1-c)+delta*rho H(c)`

therefore cannot obtain the Stage 4-J positive-stake/positive-screening result from any nonnegative additive deterministic output technology. Under a regular SOC,

`dc*/drho` has the sign of `H'(c*)`, hence is weakly negative.

## Why the Bayesian model differs

In the latent-type Bayesian model the feedback from selected customers is not additive type-by-type because the manufacturer does not observe `z`. Screening changes the mixture experiment itself. In the canonical family,

`I(c)=kappa*t^2*c^2(1-c)`

and

`I'(c)=kappa*t^2*c(2-3c)>0`

for the entire equilibrium interval `1/2<c*<2/3`.

The sign reversal is therefore unavailable under the authorized strong deterministic replacement.

## Type-observed cross-check

Once `z` becomes observed, information also becomes additive:

`I_obs(c)=integral_c^1 j(z) dz`, with `j(z)>=0`,

so `I_obs'(c)<=0`.

This is the same monotonic structure as deterministic output. The positive full-model composition effect requires a latent mixture Bayesian experiment.

## Generic-replacement verdict

`PASS — GENERIC SELECTION / OUTPUT REDUCTION DOES NOT REPRODUCE B-P1`.

This is a Stage 4 information-essentiality result, not a novelty certification. A richer non-information model with nonadditive complementarities could of course generate other quantity/composition tradeoffs; Stage 4-J claims only that the theorem is not reproduced by the strong additive type-dependent task/output replacement authorized ex ante by Stage 3-J.

## Hostile qualification

The defense must not be overstated. The canonical Bayesian experiment is Blackwell-improving in `c`, so the algebra is a quantity-versus-experiment-quality tradeoff. A future Stage 6 referee may still argue that this is an obvious application of standard experiment ordering plus a strategic screening choice. Stage 4 therefore routes any GO to a fresh proposition-level novelty re-kill rather than treating the generic-replacement PASS as publication-level novelty.
